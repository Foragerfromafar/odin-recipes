Pear and Gorgonzola Cheese Pizza

Pear and Gorgonzola pizza is a classic gourmet pizza bianca (white pizza) that pairs the crisp sweetness of fresh pears with the sharp, salty punch of creamy blue cheese

Ingredients

  • 1 (16 ounce) package refrigerated pizza crust dough
  • 4 ounces sliced provolone cheese
  • 1 Bosc pear, thinly sliced
  • 2 ½ ounces Gorgonzola cheese, crumbled
  • 2 ounces chopped walnuts
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh chives

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 450 degrees F (230 degrees C).
  2. Place pizza crust dough on a medium baking sheet. Layer with provolone cheese. Top cheese with Bosc pear slices. Sprinkle with Gorgonzola cheese and walnuts.
  3. Bake in the preheated oven until cheese is melted and crust is lightly browned, 8 to 10 minutes. Remove from heat.
